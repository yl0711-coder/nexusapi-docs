## Need help?

If you have a service group, report the issue there. Otherwise, contact the person who set up your account. Before setup, you can send your app and intended model to confirm the right group.

Send support:

- The app you use.
- The time, model and group.
- The error text or screenshot, plus a Request ID if available.

**Hide complete API keys in screenshots. Never send passwords or verification codes.** Do not guess missing details; start with the error you can see.
