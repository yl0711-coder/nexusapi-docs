## 2. Before you start

1. Have your own [API key](/en/guide/api-key) ready.
2. Copy the address and model name from the matching guide. Do not mix configurations from different apps.
3. Back up existing settings first. Do not delete the whole configuration folder.

App not listed? Send support its name and a screenshot of its settings. Hide the key.
