**A multiplier is not a discount rate.** For example, `1.3x` does not mean paying 13% of the official price. Start with the table below.

## 1. Check a discount example

**Example only: CNY 100 buys `$100` in credits; the same model has the same platform `1x` and official unit price; USD 1 is estimated at CNY 7.3.**

For usage that costs CNY 100 at the official price:

<div className="nexusapi-discount-example">

| Multiplier | Price share | You pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `0.7x` | 10% | CNY 10 |
| `1.2x` | 16% | CNY 16 |
| `1.3x` | 18% | CNY 18 |
| `5.6x` | 77% | CNY 77 |

</div>

Different top-up rates, plans or model prices change these figures. Send support your model and group to confirm your actual discount.

## 2. Find your group

Open [API Keys](https://nexusapi.link/keys) and check the **Group** of the key you use.

Open [Model Square](https://nexusapi.link/pricing), select that same group, and check your model's price.

**The selected group's price already includes its multiplier. Do not multiply it again.**

## 3. Check what a request cost

Open [Usage Logs](https://nexusapi.link/usage-logs/common), find the request, and check **Cost**.

For example, if **CNY 100 bought `$100` in credits**, a **`$0.10`** charge costs about **CNY 0.10**.

If you bought credits at a different rate or used a plan, send support the log and top-up order. **You do not need to work through formulas.** Hide keys and sensitive payment details in screenshots.
